# Northstar Legal Services

## Organization Overview

Northstar Legal Services is a fictional law firm created
for an independent IT Helpdesk and Incident Management
portfolio project.

The simulated organization supports legal professionals
and administrative employees across five departments.

## Company Structure

Total Employees: 10

Departments:
- Corporate Law
- Immigration
- Human Resources
- Accounting
- Administration

## IT Environment

The simulated technology environment consists of:

- Windows 11 employee workstations
- Microsoft 365 applications
- Outlook email
- Microsoft Word and Excel
- Network-connected printers
- Monitors, keyboards, mice, and headsets
- Zoom video conferencing
- Employee accounts and access permissions
- Shared business documents

## IT Support Responsibilities

The IT Helpdesk Technician is responsible for:

1. Receiving employee IT support requests.
2. Categorizing and prioritizing incidents.
3. Investigating reported problems.
4. Performing appropriate troubleshooting.
5. Documenting all investigation activities.
6. Resolving incidents when possible.
7. Escalating complex incidents.
8. Verifying successful resolutions.
9. Maintaining incident records.
10. Monitoring service performance.

## Project Scope

This project simulates a Level 1 IT Helpdesk environment.

All employees, incidents, equipment identifiers,
and organizational records are fictional.

No real company systems, employee accounts,
or confidential information are involved.

## Project Objectives

- Demonstrate IT incident management fundamentals.
- Practice structured troubleshooting.
- Develop ticket documentation skills.
- Establish escalation procedures.
- Analyze service desk performance.
- Build a publicly accessible technical portfolio.
