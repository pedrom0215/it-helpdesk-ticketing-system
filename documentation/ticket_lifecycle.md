
# IT Helpdesk Ticket Lifecycle

## 1. Incident Identification
An employee reports a technical problem affecting
their workstation, software, hardware, or access.

## 2. Ticket Creation
The issue is recorded with a unique ticket number,
employee information, date, description, and category.

## 3. Categorization and Prioritization
The technician determines the affected technology,
business impact, and urgency.

## 4. Investigation and Diagnosis
The technician gathers information, reproduces
the issue when appropriate, and investigates
potential causes.

## 5. Resolution or Escalation
The technician performs approved troubleshooting
or transfers the issue to an appropriate specialist.

## 6. Verification
The technician confirms that the issue has been
resolved and the employee can perform the
original business task.

## 7. Documentation and Closure
The technician records the final resolution,
relevant findings, and closure information.

## Important Principles

- Document every meaningful troubleshooting action.
- Never record passwords or authentication secrets.
- Do not make unauthorized system changes.
- Do not close tickets without verifying resolution.
- Escalate security, infrastructure, and high-impact
  incidents according to the defined procedure.
