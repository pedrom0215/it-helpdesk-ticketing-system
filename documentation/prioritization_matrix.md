
# IT Helpdesk Priority Matrix

All response targets below are simulated for this project.

| Priority | Impact | Urgency | Response Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Major business disruption or suspected security incident | Immediate | 15 minutes |
| P2 High | Important business functionality unavailable | High | 1 hour |
| P3 Medium | Individual functionality impaired | Moderate | 4 hours |
| P4 Low | Minor inconvenience or routine request | Low | 1 business day |

## Prioritization Guidelines

P1:
- Suspected account compromise
- Major infrastructure outage
- Multiple employees unable to work

P2:
- Important application unavailable
- Employee unable to access essential resources

P3:
- Individual printer or peripheral issue
- Application performance problems

P4:
- Routine software questions
- Non-urgent configuration requests

## Important

Priority is based on both business impact
and urgency, not simply the technology involved.
